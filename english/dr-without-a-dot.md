## "Dr" vs "Dr."

Writing "Dr" without a dot is standard in British and Commonwealth English, while American English uses a dot ("Dr.").

* **British English** leaves out the full stop for *contractions*: the short form starts with the first letter ("D") and ends with the last letter ("r") of the full word "doctor". Same for `Mr`, `Mrs`, `St`.
* **British English** keeps the full stop for *abbreviations* that cut the word off early: `Prof.`, `Rev.`
* **American English** uses a period for almost all title abbreviations, regardless of the final letter: `Dr.`, `Mr.`, `Mrs.`, `Prof.`
